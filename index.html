How Wolf works

Each hole

  1. The wolf tees off last. Wolf rotates in tee order.
  2. After each player’s tee shot, the wolf can pick that player or pass. Once you pass, you can’t go back.
  3. Pass on all three and you’re the lone wolf.
  4. Teams play best ball. The lower score on a team counts.

Points

2Wolf and partner win: 2 each to the wolf team
2Wolf and partner lose: 2 each to the other two
4Lone wolf wins: 4 to the wolf
2Lone wolf loses: 2 each to the other three
0Tie: no points

Wolf order

Holes 1 to 16 rotate in tee order: player 1, 2, 3, 4, then repeat.

Holes 17 and 18: the player in last place is the wolf. If tied for last, normal rotation decides among the tied players.

Using this app

Pick the partner or Lone wolf, then tap who won. Tap a hole number in the scorecard to fix a past hole.
